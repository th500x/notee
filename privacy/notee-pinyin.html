Mini Pinyin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Mini Pinyin (极拼音) is an offline Pinyin keyboard. The developer does not run an account for this app, and does not collect, sell, or upload what you type.

What stays on your device

Keystrokes are handled on the device. Words you pick, emoji counts, and keyboard settings (simplified or traditional, 26-key or 9-key, Chinese or English, key click) stay in the app’s private storage. They are not backed up to the cloud and are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

Clipboard

On Android 13 and later, while this is the active keyboard, the app keeps the last five plain-text copies on the device. It does not keep pictures, clips the system marks as sensitive, a clip that arrives while a password field is open, or an exact copy of the text the keyboard just inserted. A clip is ignored when it is longer than 500 Chinese characters or 1,000 English letters. On Android 12 and earlier the clipboard history is off.

Password fields and private browsing

In a password field the keyboard types letters, digits, and symbols directly. It does not offer candidates, learn words, open the clipboard or emoji, do arithmetic, or play a key sound. When a field asks the keyboard not to learn (private browsing, and some banking apps), words and emoji counts from that field are not saved. Clipboard history already stored is left as it is, and the clipboard page does not open in that field.

What this app does not do

There are no ads and no analytics. The app does not use the internet, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

极拼音隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

极拼音是离线拼音输入法。开发者不提供账号，也不收集、出售或上传你打的字。

留在本机的内容

按键在本机处理。选过的词、表情次数，以及简繁、26 键或 9 键、中文或英文、按键音，都存在应用自己的存储里。不备份到云端，也不发给开发者。卸载或清除数据后，这些就没有了。

剪贴板

Android 13 及以后，在本输入法正在使用时，本机保留最近五条纯文本。不保留图片、系统标成敏感的内容、密码框打开时新复制的一条，以及刚由键盘上屏的那一段原文。超过 500 个汉字或 1000 个英文字母的内容不保留。Android 12 及更早不提供剪贴板历史。

密码框和无痕

密码框里只直接打出字母、数字和符号。没有候选，不学习，不打开剪贴板和表情，不算算术，也不发按键音。输入框要求键盘不要学习时（无痕，以及部分银行应用），这个框里的选词和表情次数不保存。已经记下的剪贴历史留着，这个框里不打开剪贴板页。

本应用不做的事

没有广告，没有统计。不使用网络，也不申请定位、通讯录、相机或麦克风。

Notee Tv Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Notee Tv is a local video and audio player with one website you choose. The developer does not run an account for this app, and does not collect, sell, or upload your files.

Files on your device

Videos and audio stay on the device or on a drive you plug in. The app reads the list the Android media library already has, so it can show and play those files. It does not upload them.

The website you enter

You can save one website address on the device. The built-in browser opens that site. Anything you type there, including a username or password for that site, is sent to that website. It is not sent to the Notee Tv developer, and the app does not store that username or password.

Cookies for that site stay inside the app on the device. To remove the saved address and those cookies, clear the app’s storage in the device settings, or change the address in the app.

What this app does not do

There are no ads and no analytics. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Notee Go Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Notee Go is a party app: bar games, a record of the sitting, one sentence a day, song names, and the day’s news. The developer runs the account and the servers at notee.vip. Data sent to those servers goes over HTTPS. We do not sell personal information.

Account

Opening One Line creates an account on our server. The phone keeps a random id for that install. It is not the Android advertising id, and it is not the Android device id. You can later pick a short login id and a password, so the same account can be opened on another phone. The password is stored in a form we cannot read back.

You can also sign in with Google. We keep only Google’s account id for that sign-in. We do not ask Google for your email address, name, or profile photo, and we do not ask for access to Drive or any other Google product.

The profile you set can include a nickname, a flag, a gender choice, and one of the avatars drawn for the app. The avatar is not a photo of you. The flag is the one you pick. The app does not read your GPS location.

If the account has a short id, Settings → account has Delete account. That releases the short id, the password, and the Google link immediately, and it deletes rankings and the check-in crops below. Posts are removed on the next cleanup. An automatic account that never got a short id is removed after 30 days without use, together with the posts on it.

One Line

Sentences you post, resonances, reports, and blocks are stored on our server. Other people can see a post. A post has no photo, no precise location, and no private message. The flag shown on a post is the one in your profile.

Translation is optional and can be turned off. When it is on, a title or sentence that is not already translated on the phone is sent to our server, which sends that text to Google Cloud Translation. Text already translated stays on the phone and is not sent again. The original stays on our server.

Today’s check

The original photo stays on the phone. It is not uploaded. A square crop of that photo can be uploaded so the same account can see it on another phone. That crop is not shown in One Line and is not shown to other people. Deleting that sitting, clearing the history, or deleting the account deletes the crop.

A text card for the sitting (table name, length, mood) can be posted to One Line. It has no photo. The place, if you write one, is typed. The app does not read the photo’s location.

Game scores

When a party ends, scores from Memory Flip, Candy Spots, and Candy Hour can be sent to our server: the game, the result, the time, how many people were at the table, and the profile flag and gender. Rows for other people at the table have no nickname and no install id. Your own row is anonymous unless you turn the leaderboard on. The leaderboard is off until you turn it on. Turning it on adds your nickname and the random install id, tied to the account. You can turn score sharing off, and you can delete your ranking while the leaderboard is on. Deleting the account deletes that ranking too.

Scan & Play

Phones at the same table can connect over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. The app does not ask for location, and it does not ask for the camera. The QR code is read by Google Play services.

Two of the games, when played online, pass the nickname, flag, avatar id, and the moves through our server. That pass is only in memory, for the length of the room. It is not written into the database and it is not given to another company.

Song names, lyrics, and news

Song names are in the app. Lyrics are read from a folder you choose on the phone. Those files are not uploaded. News is loaded from our site for the city the app is showing. That request does not include the account or a GPS location.

Ads

Starting a party can show one Google rewarded ad, unless a free start is still unused or a Party Pass is on the account. The ad is from Google AdMob. Google may use the advertising id and information about that ad. Where Google requires it, the app asks for consent before requesting an ad. If you decline, close the message, or the ad does not load, the party does not start. We do not run a separate analytics package.

Purchases

Party Pass is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We do not receive the card number. We store which pass the account has, so the same account keeps it on another phone.

What this app does not do

It does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, or the camera. It does not collect an email address. It does not sell personal information.

Notee Go 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

Notee Go 是聚会用的应用：酒桌游戏、这一场的记录、每天一句、歌名，以及当天的新闻。账号和服务器由开发者放在 notee.vip。发到这些服务器的内容走 HTTPS。我们不出售个人信息。

账号

打开今日一句时，会在我们的服务器上建一个账号。手机上留一把这台安装自己的随机号。它不是广告 ID，也不是 Android 的设备号。之后可以选一个短号并设密码，换一部手机还能进同一个账号。密码以我们读不回来的形式保存。

也可以用 Google 登录。我们只保存 Google 给这个登录的账号 ID。不向 Google 要邮箱、姓名或头像，也不申请云端硬盘或其他 Google 产品的权限。

你设的资料可以有昵称、一面旗、一个性别选项，以及应用里画好的一个头像。这个头像不是你的照片。旗是你自己选的。应用不读定位。

已经有短号的账号，在设置的账户里可以删号。短号、密码和 Google 绑定会马上放开，排名和下面的打卡裁图也会删掉。帖子在下一次清理时删掉。从来没设短号的自动账号，30 天没用就会和它上面的帖子一起删掉。

今日一句

发出的句子、共鸣、举报和拉黑存在我们的服务器上。别人看得到帖子。帖子里没有照片，没有精确定位，也没有私信。帖子上的旗是资料里选的那一面。

翻译可以关。开着的时候，手机上还没译过的标题或句子会发到我们的服务器，再交给 Google 的翻译服务。已经译过的留在手机上，不再发送。原文仍在我们的服务器上。

今日打卡

照片原片留在手机上，不上传。可以上传一张正方形裁图，给同一个账号换手机时看自己的记录。这张裁图不进今日一句，也不给别人看。删掉这一场、清掉历史，或删掉账号，裁图会一起删掉。

这一场的文字卡（桌名、时长、心情）可以发到今日一句，里面没有照片。地点如果写了，是手写的。应用不读照片里的定位。

游戏成绩

聚会结束时，记忆翻牌、糖点和糖果三消的成绩可以发到我们的服务器：哪一局、结果、用时、同桌人数，以及资料里的旗和性别。同桌其他人的那一行没有昵称，也没有安装号。你自己的那一行默认不带身份，除非打开排行榜。排行榜默认关。打开之后，会加上你的昵称和这把随机安装号，并记在账号上。可以关掉成绩分享。排行榜开着时，可以删掉自己的排名。删号也会删掉这个排名。

扫码对局

同一张桌子上的手机可以用蓝牙和 Wi-Fi 连起来。应用不申请定位，也不申请相机。二维码由 Google Play 服务读取。

其中两款在网上玩时，昵称、旗、头像编号和对局会经过我们的服务器。只在内存里转发，房间结束就没有了。不写入数据库，也不交给别的公司。

歌名、歌词和新闻

歌名在应用里。歌词从你在手机上选的文件夹读取，不上传。新闻从我们的网站按应用正在显示的城市读取。这个请求不带账号，也不带定位。

广告

开始聚会时可能看一条 Google 激励广告。这一次免费开始还没用掉，或者账号上有 Party Pass，就不看。广告来自 Google AdMob。Google 可能使用广告 ID，以及这条广告的相关信息。在 Google 要求的地方，应用会先询问是否同意，再请求广告。拒绝、关掉，或广告没加载出来，聚会不会开始。我们没有另外再装一套统计工具。

购买

Party Pass 通过 Google Play 购买。付款由 Google 处理。我们收不到卡号。我们只记下这个账号买的是哪一档，换手机还能用。

本应用不做的事

不申请定位、通讯录、麦克风或相机。不收集邮箱。不出售个人信息。

联系
